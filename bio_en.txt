

I’m currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science (CIC), at the University of Brasília (UnB). From 2024 to 2026, I was a maître de conférences at the University Toulouse Capitole, France, at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse, (IRIT), and part of the REVA team.

I did a 18 month-long postdoc research working with histopathology image analysis for cancer treatment with Genetic Programming in the IRIT@CRCT group, a joint laboratory between the Cancer Research Center of Toulouse CRCT and IRIT, together with prof. Sylvain Cussat-Blanc.

I got my PhD degree (also my masters) from the University of Tsukuba, Japan. I was part of the Autoadaptation in Multi-Objective Optimization group, under Prof. Claus Aranha supervision. I obtained my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from UnB in 2016, where I was supervised by prof. Marcelo Ladeira.

My research interests are related to Computational Intelligence, such as Evolutionary Computation and Artificial Life, with a greater focus on multi-objective optimization and Genetic Programming. Overall, I’m interested in programs that can adapt themselves, in applications of Evolutionary Computation (interpretability, biomedical data, games), as well as more speculative use of these Computational Intelligence for Artificial Life (such as the evolution of virtual creatures and the worlds where the live).
